How to use this book

400 ready-to-generate prompts. 40 episodes × 10 scenes. Each prompt is one 10-second vertical (9:16) clip of Saapwaa’s comedy vlog from his everyday life in Narak, with the Hindi dialogue already inside the prompt.

  • Copy the whole prompt box into your video generator (Google Flow / Veo or similar). Every prompt is under 900 characters.
  • Play the 10 scenes of an episode in order for a 100-second short, or post any scene alone as a reel.
  • Every prompt repeats Saapwaa’s full look, so each one works on its own.
  • If a clip comes out wrong: generate again, or delete the dialogue sentence, generate it silent and add the voice in your editor.
  • No prompt can guarantee a perfect result; it depends on the tool, its version and its settings.
  • The plain-text file sent with this PDF has the same 400 prompts. Copying Hindi from a PDF can garble it, so copy from the text file when you can.

A note on respect

All homes, shops, people and rules here are fictional comedy. The comedy comes from Saapwaa’s daily inconveniences, never from mocking Yama, Chitragupta, deities or anyone’s faith, and no scene shows punishment. A few scenes visit places described in the Garuda Purana (Saumya with its fig tree and the Pushpabhadra river, the cold city Sheetadhya, the Vaitarani seen from afar), and those scenes keep a respectful tone.
