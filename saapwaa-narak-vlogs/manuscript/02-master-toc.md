## Detailed chapter plan

### Part II — Narak in the traditional sources (30 pp.)

1. What the Garuda Purana is, and why the Preta Khaṇḍa matters here (2 pp.)
2. The sources used, their editions and how to cite them (3 pp.)
3. The road to Yama: distance, conditions and the sixteen cities (6 pp.)
4. The Vaitarani: description, name, crossing and the gift of a cow (5 pp.)
5. Yama’s city: four gates, the court, Chitragupta and the Śravaṇas (5 pp.)
6. The named narakas, described briefly and soberly (3 pp.)
7. Where translations disagree (numbers, names, chapter order) (3 pp.)
8. What the sources do not describe — and why that is where the comedy lives (3 pp.)

### Part III — Character bible and world continuity (15 pp.)

Saapwaa’s locked design and turnaround notes (3) · voice and vocabulary (2) · props (1) · six recurring characters with locks (4) · Dhuan Tola map and locations (2) · timeline and running-joke ledger (3).

### Parts IV–VII — The world (110 pp.)

Each location chapter follows the same pattern: what the place is, its label ({SRC}, {INT} or {FIC}), its look (a reusable environment lock for prompts), who lives or works there, what is funny about it, what is off-limits, and two or three ready scenes that can be dropped into any episode.

### Part VIII — Comedy (20 pp.)

The engagement framework (hook → curiosity → interaction → payoff → continuation), with worked examples from the library; ten comedy engines used in the series and the episodes that use them; roasting rules; dialogue rhythm for ten-second clips.

### Part IX — Planning (15 pp.)

Episode list with themes and comedy engines; the day counter; scene register; continuity checklist; a template for planning a new episode.

### Part X — Production library (80 pp.)

| Episodes | Theme block | Scenes (approx.) |
|---|---|---:|
| 1–5 | Home life: morning, room tour, tea, workshop, cooking | 27 |
| 6–10 | Out in Dhuan Tola: signal, bench, maze, interview, bargaining | 24 |

| 16–20 | Source-inspired trips and reviews: Saumya, Vaitarani viewpoint, accommodation review, serious advice, bed hacks | 24 |
| 21–25 | Payoffs: crow story, fitness, oil crisis, a guest returns, Day 30 special | 22 |

### Part XI — Workflow and references (20 pp.)

Workflow from script to edit (4) · consistency techniques (3) · troubleshooting table (4) · voice-over and subtitles in the edit (2) · bibliography and source log (4) · index of scenes, places and characters (3).
