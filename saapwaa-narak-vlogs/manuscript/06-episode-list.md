# 5 · The Episode List

*Twenty-five episodes, each built on a different comedy engine so the series never leans on one kind of joke. Each episode is a 40–60 second short made of five or six 10-second scenes.*

## Comedy engines used in the series

| Code | Engine | What drives the laugh |
|---|---|---|
| CR | Confident routine undermined | Saapwaa presents a polished routine; the world refuses to cooperate. |
| PP | Professional presentation | He acts like a real-estate agent, chef or reviewer; the facts embarrass him. |
| VA | Vendor’s unexpected answer | A resident’s calm logic beats his question. |
| OI | Over-engineered invention | A serious inventor solves a tiny problem in a huge way. |
| PG | Plan goes wrong | A clear goal, an escalating series of setbacks. |
| AI | Awkward interview | He wants content; the resident gives unusable, perfect answers. |
| SL | Social-rule discovery | A harmless local custom he did not know about. |
| BG | Bargaining | A negotiation where the stakes are absurdly small. |
| SD | Sensory deprivation | Comedy in darkness or silence; reactions carry the scene. |
| GT | Gentle trip | Wonder first, small jokes second (used for source-inspired places). |
| CB | Callback payoff | A running joke finally lands. |

## Why the list looks like this

- **Variety of engines.** No engine runs in two consecutive episodes except where a payoff needs it (Episodes 20–21).
- **Queues, offices and heat are rationed.** Office humour appears once (Ep 13), queue humour once (Ep 14), and heat is the main engine only in Episodes 1, 5 and 20.
- **Source-inspired episodes are spaced out** (6, 15, 16, 17) and are written in a gentler, “wonder first” register.
- **Payoffs are back-loaded** so that Episodes 20–25 reward viewers who have followed along.

## Episode synopses for this batch

**Episode 1 — Good Morning, Narak (Day 6).** Saapwaa runs his now-familiar morning: waking on the griddle-hot bed, drinking “cold” water that is nearly tea, refilling the lamp, testing the bath trickle, losing breakfast to Kallu, and asking Tapu Chacha — again — for his bucket.

**Episode 2 — Cave No. 13 Room Tour (Day 7).** He gives a polished estate-agent tour of his cave. The ceiling drops a pebble, the bed burns his tail, the wet-cloth hack turns into a papad, the kitchen cooks without fire, and the bathroom tour is cancelled on dignity grounds.

**Episode 3 — The Truth About Cold Tea (Day 8).** Rumour says Garam Adda sells cold tea. Kulhad Didi explains the local definition of cold, sets a price, and Saapwaa reviews a cup of tea that tries to melt his face. Kallu is watching his biscuit.
