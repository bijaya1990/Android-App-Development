Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

EMI Calculator ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and maintained to help you plan Car, Home, Personal, and Bike loan EMIs. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. By using the App, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information Collection

EMI Calculator does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, or phone number. Your loan calculations happen entirely on your device. The App does, however, display advertising through Google AdMob, which involves some automated data collection described in Section 3 below.

2. Data Usage — Calculator Data

All figures you enter — loan amount, interest rate, tenure, down payment, and total price — are used solely on your device to compute your EMI, amortization schedule, and PDF reports. This data is stored only in your device's local memory and app cache for the duration of your session and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Google AdMob

The App displays banner and interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partner networks may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, device and app information, general location (derived from IP address), and ad interaction data, in accordance with Google's Partner Sites policy. This data is used to serve ads and is not linked to your loan calculation inputs, which never leave your device. You can review or reset your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalized advertising, from your device's system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android versions).

4. Firebase Analytics

This version of the App does not use Firebase Analytics or any other analytics SDK. No usage analytics or behavioral tracking data is collected beyond what Google AdMob itself requires to serve ads, as described above.

5. Crash Reporting

This version of the App does not use a crash-reporting service such as Firebase Crashlytics. If crash reporting is added in a future release, this section will be updated to explain what diagnostic data is collected and how it is used to fix issues.

6. Third-Party Services

Besides Google AdMob (Section 3), the App does not embed any third-party SDKs that transmit data off your device. Locally bundled open-source libraries (such as AndroidX and MPAndroidChart) run entirely on-device and do not communicate with external servers. See Settings → Open Source Libraries for the full list.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose financial calculator not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App serves ads via Google AdMob, it is not intended for use by children, consistent with Google Play's Families policies and AdMob's requirements around child-directed treatment.

8. Security

Because your financial inputs never leave your device, there is no server-side transmission or storage of that data for us to secure. PDF reports you generate are saved to your device's app-specific storage and are only shared when you explicitly choose to share, print, or export them.

9. Your Rights

You may clear all data associated with the App at any time by clearing the App's storage/cache from your device's Settings, or by uninstalling the App. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier at any time from your device's system settings. Since we do not hold your calculator data on our servers, there is no remote data for us to delete, correct, or export on your behalf.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notified within the App.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@naukripatra.in.
