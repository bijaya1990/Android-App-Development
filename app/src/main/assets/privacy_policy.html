Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

EMI Calculator ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and maintained to help you plan Car, Home, Personal, and Bike loan EMIs. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. By using the App, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Information Collection

EMI Calculator is designed to work fully offline. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information through the App itself.

2. Data Usage — Calculator Data

All figures you enter — loan amount, interest rate, tenure, down payment, and total price — are used solely on your device to compute your EMI, amortization schedule, and PDF reports. This data is stored only in your device's local memory and app cache for the duration of your session and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Google AdMob

This version of the App does not integrate Google AdMob or display any advertisements. Should a future update introduce ads, this Privacy Policy will be revised in advance to disclose what advertising identifiers or data are involved, and the updated policy will be made available before the change takes effect.

4. Firebase Analytics

This version of the App does not use Firebase Analytics or any other analytics SDK. No usage analytics, behavioral data, or device identifiers are collected or transmitted.

5. Crash Reporting

This version of the App does not use a crash-reporting service such as Firebase Crashlytics. If crash reporting is added in a future release, this section will be updated to explain what diagnostic data is collected and how it is used to fix issues.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not embed any third-party SDKs that transmit data off your device. Locally bundled open-source libraries (such as AndroidX and MPAndroidChart) run entirely on-device and do not communicate with external servers. See Settings → Open Source Libraries for the full list.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose financial calculator not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect personal information from any user, this applies equally to users of all ages.

8. Security

Because your financial inputs never leave your device, there is no server-side transmission or storage for us to secure. PDF reports you generate are saved to your device's app-specific storage and are only shared when you explicitly choose to share, print, or export them.

9. Your Rights

You may clear all data associated with the App at any time by clearing the App's storage/cache from your device's Settings, or by uninstalling the App. Since we do not hold any of your data on our servers, there is no remote data for us to delete, correct, or export on your behalf.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notified within the App.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@naukripatra.in.
