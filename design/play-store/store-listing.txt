===== APP NAME (max 30) =====
NaukriPatra: Latest Job Update

===== SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80) =====
Latest job openings across India & USA, by your state and qualification.

===== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) =====
NaukriPatra helps you find your dream job. Browse the latest job openings from across India and the USA in a simple, easy-to-read format – number of posts, qualification, age limit, salary, fees, last date and official links, all in one place.

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
NaukriPatra is an independent, privately owned job information app. It is NOT a government app and does NOT represent any government entity. NaukriPatra is not affiliated with, authorised by or endorsed by any government department, ministry, commission, recruitment board, university or public institution.

Job information in the app is collected from publicly available official notifications and official websites, for general information only. Every job page shows its official source. Please verify all details – eligibility, dates, fees and how to apply – on the official website or notification before you apply.

KEY FEATURES
• Latest jobs updated every day
• Jobs for your state – choose your state from a dropdown on every job list
• Jobs for your qualification – 8th, 10th, 12th, ITI, Diploma, Graduate, Post Graduate, B.Tech
• All India jobs and USA jobs
• Search by post, department or exam name
• Clear job details: posts, salary, age limit, qualification and last date
• "Days left" countdown so you never miss a last date
• Official website and notification PDF links on every job
• Save jobs and see which ones close this week
• Job alert notifications (can be turned off anytime)
• Dark mode, and works on phones and tablets
• No login or sign-up needed

WHAT NAUKRIPATRA DOES NOT DO
NaukriPatra does not conduct exams, accept applications, collect any fee, or hire on behalf of any organisation. To apply for a job you always go to the organisation's own official website. NaukriPatra never asks for money to get you a job.
